# Applying animation to a joint

Revolute and slider joints can be driven through their range from the Joints panel. Nothing is written to the file: the animation is a viewport tool.

## In the viewport

1. In the **Joints** panel, right-click the *revolute* row and choose **Animate…**.

   ![Joint row menu](/img/docs/ui/joints-context-menu.png)

2. The animate bar appears at the bottom of the viewport: **Start** and **End** angles, **Steps**, and a **Playback** mode. The start and end are seeded from the joint's `.limits()`, 0° and 180° here; a joint without limits animates one full turn (a slider travels 10 units from where it is).

   ![Animate bar](/img/docs/ui/animate-bar.png)

3. Set **Playback** to *Reciprocate* and click **Play**. The swing leaf closes onto the fixed leaf and opens again; *Loop* restarts from Start each time, *Single* stops at End.

   ![The hinge closing](/img/docs/ui/animate-bar-playing.png)

4. **Play** toggles to **Pause** while running. **Stop** returns the leaf to where it was when the bar opened, and **✕** closes the bar.

The same drive is available on slider joints, in document units of travel along the joint's Z axis. Editing Start or End while paused restarts the sweep from Start on the next Play.
