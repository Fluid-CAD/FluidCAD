# Ellipse

An ellipse with its axes aligned to the sketch plane: cams, oval bosses, and the elongated holes a slot is too square for.

## In the viewport

1. Click **Ellipse** on the sketch toolbar (shortcut `el`).
2. Click the centre. The **X / Y** pill places it exactly; a centre that snaps onto a vertex or the origin gets a coincident written for it.
3. Move the cursor out: its horizontal offset from the centre is **RX**, its vertical offset **RY**. Click or press Enter to take the RX pill, then again for RY — or type either value (a number, a variable, or an expression).

The radii land in the statement as written; they are not dimensions the solver can drive, so there is no dimension row to double-click afterwards. Retype the number, or bind it to a [`param()`](/docs/part/param), to resize.

## By hand

```js
ellipse(center, rx, ry)
```

`rx` and `ry` are the **semi-radii** (half the width and half the height) along the plane's X and Y axes.

## Accessors

| Accessor | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `e.center()` | The centre point — the only solver entity an ellipse registers |

The centre takes every point constraint (`coincident`, `distance`, `midpoint`, `symmetric`, `fix`, a `horizontal`/`vertical` pair with another point) and shows as a draggable point in the viewport. The radii stay fixed literals: the solver positions the ellipse through its centre but never resizes it. To change the size, change the numbers or bind them to a [`param()`](/docs/part/param).

## Modifiers

```js
ellipse([0, 0], 50, 25).guide()
ellipse([0, 0], 50, 25).name('cam')
```
